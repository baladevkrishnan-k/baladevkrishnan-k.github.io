Projects

LedgerFlow – Multi-Company Billing & Accounting System

Desktop application · Business process automation

An offline desktop accounting application that manages billing, customer accounts and financial transactions independently across multiple companies, built to streamline financial operations and improve financial visibility.

  • Company-wise and financial-year-wise invoice management with configurable invoice numbering, GST and non-GST billing, product management and automated invoice calculations.
  • Integrated party ledgers that combine invoices, payments, discounts and opening balances into one account view with outstanding balances and transaction histories.
  • Financial-year opening balance carry-forward, plus company-specific customer, product and accounting records.
  • Invoice editing, backdated transactions and audit history, with automatic recalculation of related party balances and ledgers.
  • Modular architecture using PySide6, SQLAlchemy ORM and SQLite, with automated testing and local data storage for offline operation.
